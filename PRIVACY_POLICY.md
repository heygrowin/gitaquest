### Core Privacy Pledge
GitaQuest is built with spiritual reverence. The app is **100% ad-free**, does not require an account, does not track you, and does not sell or share any user data. All your reading progress and reflections remain private on your device.

---

### 1. Introduction
GitaQuest provides a digital platform for reading, studying, and contemplating the sacred scripture of the Bhagavad Gita. This Privacy Policy informs users about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when using our mobile application.

---

### 2. Data Collection and Storage
GitaQuest is built with an **offline-first and privacy-first** architecture:
* **No Account Required:** You can read, search, and contemplate all 18 chapters and 701 verses without registering or providing an email address or phone number.
* **Local On-Device Storage:** All reading positions (last read verse), bookmarks, personal reflection notes, reading streaks, and chapter progress are stored locally on your device storage (via local storage and app preferences).
* **No Tracking or Profiling:** We do not track your location, browsing habits, device identifiers (IMEI/MAC), or reading history across the internet.

---

### 3. Advertisements
GitaQuest contains **zero advertisements**. We do not integrate third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or Facebook Audience Network) and do not serve banner ads, interstitials, or video ads.

---

### 4. Voluntary Contributions & Third-Party Payment Services
GitaQuest is completely free to read without paywalls. Users who wish to support ongoing maintenance and scripture hosting may optionally choose to offer a voluntary contribution (सेवा एवं दान).
* When you tap to contribute, the app opens our verified payment provider (**Razorpay** at `https://razorpay.me/@heygrowin`) in a secure browser tab.
* All transactions, UPI payments, card payments, and net banking interactions are processed directly on Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant secure infrastructure.
* GitaQuest **never accesses, receives, or stores** your bank account details, UPI PINs, credit/debit card numbers, or payment credentials.
* Refer to [Razorpay's Privacy Policy](https://razorpay.com/privacy/) for details on their data processing.

---

### 5. Device Permissions
GitaQuest requests only minimal, necessary system permissions:
* `android.permission.INTERNET`: Used exclusively to open voluntary donation links in a secure web tab, load font typography, and enable optional certificate PDF exports.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
Our application contains sacred spiritual scripture suitable for seekers of all ages (Content Rating: PEGI 3 / Everyone). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 7. Data Retention & Deletion
Because your reading progress, notes, and bookmarks are stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over your data:
* You can clear all app data at any time through your Android Device Settings > Apps > GitaQuest > Clear Data.
* Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local data from your device.
* You can use the in-app "Data Backup" feature in Settings to export a copy of your bookmarks and notes in JSON format.
